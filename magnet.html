The load sheet that stops you buying the wrong array.

Daily watt-hours decide everything. Panels, battery, inverter — they're just math on top of what you actually run.

People shop panels first. That's backwards. A microwave for 15 minutes is 250 Wh. An induction burner for 45 minutes is 1,125 Wh. Heat is what blows solar budgets. Propane or wood wins there in 2026 economics.

Use it in 10 minutes. List every load × hours. Sum Wh/day. Array watts ≈ daily Wh ÷ sun hours ÷ 0.75. Bank Wh ≈ daily Wh × days of autonomy ÷ DoD (0.80 LiFePO4, 0.50 AGM).
