Solar or generator for off-grid power — which one?

Built by Jeremy Panasuk. Published September 13, 2026. Updated October 3, 2026.

Same daily watt-hours on both sides. The solar column is this site's calculator. The generator column is runtime and tank size from the manufacturer's own page, divided through so the fuel can be checked.

Quick answer: Battle Born's 2,400 Wh example, at 4 sun hours, 2 days of autonomy, and 80% depth of discharge, is an 800 W array and a 6,000 Wh bank (250 Ah at 24 V) in sizeSystem. Delivering that same 2,400 Wh from a Honda EU2200i at its 1,800 W rated load takes 1.333 hours and 0.3958 gallons. A Champion 201296 at half of its 3,500 W gasoline running rating takes 1.371 hours and 0.4604 gallons.

One day, two machines

The daily energy is the 2,400 watt-hour example in Battle Born's battery article, the same figure their page divides by 12 V. It is their example, not a measurement of a cabin. The calculator form opens at 4 sun hours, 2 days of autonomy, 80% depth of discharge, and 24 V. Those are the inputs below. sizeSystem sets array watts to daily watt-hours ÷ sun hours ÷ 0.75, rounded up to the next 100 W. The comment in assets/app.js describes 0.75 as wiring, controller, battery round-trip, and panel heat and soiling. The bank is daily watt-hours × days ÷ depth of discharge.

Fuel per hour is the published tank divided by the published runtime at that load. Honda does not print a separate gallons-per-hour line; 0.95 ÷ 3.2 and 0.95 ÷ 8.1 are that division. Champion's 14 hours and 10.5 hours are stated at 50% load. Half of the published running watts is the wattage used here for that phrase. Changing the load moves both the hours and the fuel, which is why the quarter-load row burns 0.6255 gallons for the same 2,400 Wh. The hour figures are those divisions rounded to three decimals. The fuel figures use the unrounded divisions and are then rounded: gallons to four decimals, propane pounds to three.

What the watt ratings do not say

Honda's maximum AC output is 2,200 W (18.3 A). The runtime of 3.2 hours is at rated load, and rated load is 1,800 W (15 A). Dividing 2,400 by 2,200 uses the maximum and does not match the runtime Honda measured. Champion lists 4,375 gasoline starting watts beside 3,500 running watts, and 3,950 propane starting watts beside 3,150 running watts. The runtime sentences are at 50% load, not at the starting-watt figures. A load above the running watts is outside the continuous rating those pages print.

On a 4-sun-hour input, 800 × 4 × 0.75 = 2,400, so the array result lands exactly on the daily energy once the calculator's 0.75 factor is included. Drop the sun-hour input to the function's 2.5-hour winter line and the printed array becomes 1,300 W. The generator rows do not change when sun hours change: they still have to make 2,400 Wh at whatever load point you choose, and they still burn the fuel in the table. That is the gap the array formula is built around. It is not a climate survey. The 2.5 hours are the number written in sizeSystem.

Noise, as each page states it

Honda's specification table lists 57 dB(A) at rated load and 48 dB(A) at 1/4 load. The same page also says the EU2200i operates at 48 to 57 dBA. It does not print a measurement distance next to those figures. Champion's description says 68 dBA from 23 feet, and the specification table lists 68 dB(A). The two pages do not describe one shared test, so 57 and 68 are the figures each manufacturer published, not a paired measurement from this site.

A common mistake

Treating a generator's running watts as if they were watt-hours. 1,800 W is Honda's rated output while the set is producing that load. 2,400 Wh is a day of energy. The bridge is hours: watt-hours ÷ watts at the load where the tank runtime was measured, then tank gallons ÷ those published hours. A gallons-per-hour figure that is not that division is not a figure from these two specification pages.

Limits

Champion's page for the 201296 says: using a generator indoors can kill you in minutes. Generator exhaust contains carbon monoxide. This is a poison you cannot see or smell. Never use a generator inside a home or garage, even if doors and windows are open. Only use it outside and far away from windows, doors, and vents. The same page says CO Shield is not a substitute for an indoor carbon monoxide alarm or for safe operation, and that engine exhaust must not enter a confined area. Generators must always be used outdoors, far away from occupied buildings, with the exhaust pointed away from people and buildings.

Honda's EU2200i page says CO-MINDER continuously monitors carbon monoxide and automatically shuts the generator down before detected levels near the generator become dangerously high. That shutdown is not a location instruction. Follow Honda's owner's manual for where the EU2200i may run. Honda lists this model as not for sale in California. Neither manufacturer's page is a sizing rule for a well pump or any other motor; the starting watts above are the only surge figures this page uses, and only for these two models.

FAQ

What does the calculator size for 2,400 Wh, and what fuel matches that day?

At 4 sun hours, 2 days, and 80% depth of discharge, sizeSystem prints 800 W and a 6,000 Wh bank, 250 Ah at 24 V. Its winter line, at 2.5 sun hours, prints 1,300 W. Honda's EU2200i at its 1,800 W rated load takes 2,400 ÷ 1,800 = 1.333 hours and (2,400 ÷ 1,800) × (0.95 ÷ 3.2) = 0.3958 gallons. Champion's 201296 at half of its 3,500 gasoline running watts takes 1.371 hours and 0.4604 gallons.

Why does the EU2200i use more fuel at quarter load for the same watt-hours?

Honda lists 8.1 hours at 1/4 load on a 0.95 gallon tank, and the rated output is 1,800 W, so that point is 450 W. Delivering 2,400 Wh at 450 W takes 2,400 ÷ 450 = 5.333 hours and (2,400 ÷ 450) × (0.95 ÷ 8.1) = 0.6255 gallons, compared with 0.3958 gallons at the 1,800 W rated point.

Where do these generators have to run?

Champion's 201296 page says using a generator indoors can kill you in minutes, that exhaust contains carbon monoxide, and that the generator must only be used outside and far from windows, doors, and vents. CO Shield is not a substitute for an indoor carbon monoxide alarm. Honda's EU2200i page says CO-MINDER shuts the generator down before detected carbon monoxide levels near the generator become dangerously high. Follow each owner's manual.
