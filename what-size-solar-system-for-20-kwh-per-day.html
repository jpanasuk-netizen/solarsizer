What size solar system for 20 kWh per day?

Quick answer: NREL PVWatts v8 shows 1 kW of south-facing panels tilted at latitude making 1,787 kWh a year in Phoenix, 1,308 in Chicago and 1,071 in Seattle. Using 20 kWh a day is 7,300 kWh a year, so plan on about 4.1 kW of panels in Phoenix, 5.6 kW in Chicago and 6.8 kW in Seattle.

How the math works

20 kWh a day × 365 days = 7,300 kWh a year. Divide that by how much 1 kW of panels makes in your city each year and you get the kW of panels. Divide kW × 1,000 by your panel's rated watts for a panel count, rounded up. PVWatts already includes its 14% loss assumption; shading, a different tilt or a different direction changes the result, so run your own address in PVWatts before you buy.
