Frequently asked questions

What size solar system do I need for an off-grid cabin?

Add the watt-hours of everything you run per day, divide by your location's worst-month sun hours, then divide by 0.86 — NREL PVWatts' default 14% system losses (soiling, shading, mismatch, wiring, availability).

How many solar panels does it take to charge a 200Ah battery?

A 200 Ah 12 V battery stores 2,400 Wh. panelsForBattery does amp-hours × volts ÷ sun hours ÷ 0.85. At 4.5 sun hours that is 2,400 ÷ 4.5 ÷ 0.85 = 627.45 W, which the screen rounds to 627 W.

MPPT or PWM charge controller — which do I need?

Victron's SmartSolar MPPT 100/30 and 100/50 datasheet says an ultra-fast MPPT controller improves energy harvest by up to 30% compared with PWM controllers, especially under a clouded sky when light intensity keeps changing. Size it on this page as array watts ÷ battery voltage ÷ 0.85, then round up to the next listed rating (10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 80, or 100 A).

What size inverter do I need for off-grid solar?

Total the continuous watts of everything that could run at once, then apply the surge factor in the inverter tab: 1× resistive, 3× motor, 4× compressor, or 2× mixed. The calculator multiplies that continuous total by 1.25. The refrigerator preset is 150 W; a compressor factor of 4 on that preset is 600 W of surge.

How does off-grid solar sizing work?

Everything flows from one number: daily watt-hours. Panels must replace that number every sun-hour day — array watts = daily Wh ÷ sun hours ÷ 0.86, the derate from NREL PVWatts' default 14% system losses (soiling, shading, mismatch, wiring, connections, light-induced degradation, nameplate, availability). It does not include inverter or battery round-trip losses. The battery bank must carry you through sunless days: bank Wh = daily Wh × days of autonomy ÷ max depth of discharge. LiFePO4's 80% usable depth is why it wins on-paper capacity math even before cycle life.
