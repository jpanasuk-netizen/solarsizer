Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SolarSizer (solar-sizer.com) is a free calculator site. We designed it to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The values you enter (loads, sun hours, battery type) are not transmitted to or stored on our servers — they exist only in your browser session. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, emails, or personal profiles through the calculator.

Affiliate links never influence the sizing math, recommendations, or content of the calculators.

Analytics & cookies

We do not run our own analytics or set our own tracking cookies. Third parties (such as Google) may set cookies as described above; you can block or delete these through your browser settings.
