Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

SolarSizer (solar-sizer.com) is a free calculator site owned by Jeremy Panasuk, New Auburn, Wisconsin. It is set up to collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

The calculator on the homepage runs in your browser (assets/app.js). The values you enter — loads, sun hours, battery type — are not transmitted to or stored on a server. They exist only in that browser session. There are no accounts, and the calculator does not ask for your name or email.

What the pages load

Pages load files from this same site: assets/styles.css on every styled page, and on the homepage assets/app.js, assets/monetization.js, and assets/magnet-cta.js. The topic map also loads assets/magnet-cta.js. The HTML does not load Google AdSense or an analytics script. The domain is served through Cloudflare, which rewrites mailto links and loads its email-decode script on those pages.

assets/monetization.js stores one sessionStorage value in your browser if you dismiss the Amazon bar, so the bar stays dismissed for that browser session. That value is not sent to us.

assets/magnet-cta.js adds a link to the cabin load sheet on this site and a link to a TikTok profile. Nothing is sent unless you follow a link.

Amazon affiliate links

Some pages contain links to Amazon.com through the Amazon Associates program, using the tag generatorsi0d-20. If you click one and buy something, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. After you leave solar-sizer.com, Amazon's site is governed by Amazon's own privacy policy and may set cookies. Affiliate links do not change the sizing math.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not run its own analytics and does not set tracking cookies. The only browser storage its scripts use is the sessionStorage value described above.
